# Marketing — MoonLauncher

## Position

Launch-as-a-service for pump.fun. The customer pays SOL. The service does
the work: identity, image, launch, optional momentum.

Why this wins:

- Competitors ask for seed phrases or hold funds. MoonLauncher signs
  nothing. The customer's wallet creates the coin. This is the main trust
  message.
- Images and metadata are produced in seconds. No downloads, no pinning
  accounts, no JSON editing.
- Boost tokens land in the customer's wallet. The customer controls the
  supply from block one.

## Customer

- Crypto-native users on X (Twitter), Telegram, and Reddit who want a coin
  but not the setup work.
- Small teams that test launch mechanics.
- The message for all of them: one theme, one signature, one live coin.

## Channels, in order

1. **X (Twitter).** Post every launch from the feed with the coin image.
   Reply to pump.fun launch chatter with the front-page link.
2. **Telegram.** pump.fun and Solana degen groups. Short demo clip: theme in,
   coin out, boost bar filling.
3. **Reddit.** r/solana, r/CryptoCurrency launch threads. Use the simple
   copy only; no hype words.
4. **SEO.** The landing page targets: "how to launch a coin on pump.fun",
   "pump.fun launch service", "create solana meme coin". The FAQ answers
   the questions people search. Sitemap and robots are live.

## Copy rules

- ASD-STE100 style: short sentences. Active voice. Present tense. No
  idioms. One idea per sentence.
- Never promise price movement. The boost is described as buys by wallets,
  not as "pumping" or "profit".
- Trust words: your keys, your coin, no custody.

## Pricing launch plan

- First 20 launches: 50% off with code GENESIS (manual refunds until the
  coupon system exists).
- After ~50 paid launches: move to 0.4 / 0.9 / 2.0 SOL.
- Later: "trending hours" upsell (~2 SOL/hour) once the feed has traffic.

## Metrics to watch

- Theme -> identity -> sign funnel drop-off (per step).
- Sign -> confirm success rate (watch for dropped launch transactions).
- Boost completion rate and time to complete.
- Repeat customers (same customer pubkey twice).

## Legal note

The FAQ states: tool, not advice, payments final. Add a terms page before
any paid marketing push.
